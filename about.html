About SmartJobs

We built SmartJobs because job hunting as a student shouldn't mean wading through listings meant for people with ten years of experience.

Our Mission

SmartJobs connects students and freshers with internships and entry-level roles that actually match their skill level — no "3+ years required" fine print. Every listing is filtered for fresher-friendly experience requirements.

Whether you're looking for your first internship or your first full-time job, we want your search to feel possible, not overwhelming.

What We Value

Students First

Every feature we build starts with "does this help a student get hired?"

Transparency

Real salary ranges and clear experience requirements on every listing.

No Gatekeeping

Entry-level should mean entry-level. We work to keep it that way.
